04 / METHODOLOGY

Evidence is part
of the story.

Use this atlas as a starting point for research. Read the linked reporting before drawing conclusions.

Full editorial methodology ↗
01

Describe, attribute, verify.

Records distinguish participants’ demands, disputed claims, and authorities’ responses. A source link supports a claim; it does not make that claim undisputed. Verification notes explain what remains uncertain.

02

Intensity has more than one dimension.

A large peaceful gathering differs from a small event with severe violence. Turnout estimates retain their source and uncertainty. Disruption, violence, and state action are never collapsed into a single score.

03

Recency is a condition, not a promise.

“Reported ongoing” requires a source observation within the last 72 hours. Older ongoing records become “Needs review.” Future planned events remain planned. A record marked “Ended” describes the recorded event, not the end of a wider movement.

04

Silence is a coverage gap.

No published record means we have not established coverage. News availability, language, access restrictions, and editorial capacity can all create uneven visibility. Locations are shown at the precision supported by the source.
